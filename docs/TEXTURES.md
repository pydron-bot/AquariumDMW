# Texture photos wanted

Every texture is currently painted procedurally by `blender/lib/textures.py`.
Real photos of the real things are the biggest single upgrade available.
Each photo below replaces one generator.

## How to shoot them (applies to all)

- **Turn the RGB/purple LEDs off.** Daylight or plain white light only: a
  window on an overcast day is ideal. Colored light bakes into the texture.
- **Shoot straight on**, with the phone parallel to the surface, filling
  the frame. No flash: it leaves a hotspot.
- **Put a ruler or coin in one shot of each** so I can get the scale right.
- Full-resolution originals (AirDrop or Files), not screenshots. Several
  per item is better than one.

Trimming time is the natural moment: a leaf cut off each plant costs
nothing.
